# Visualize the direction

When the harness has native image generation, the locked direction is visualized before it is built. This step is mandatory when image generation exists; it is the proven way to get the most compositional and ambitious work.

## The comp round

1. Render **three compositional options** for the first viewport: the same content, the same chosen world, three different arrangements (hierarchy, topology, density). The options differ in composition, not palette or type; those are already committed.
2. Put the three comps before the user with a one-line thesis per option. The user picks one or asks for a variant.
3. The approved comp is the contract. Phase one of the build is reproduction (see [new-work.md](new-work.md)): rebuild the comp at its own breakpoint until the render overlaps it near pixel-perfectly. The comp outranks every written record of it.
4. When the comp does not cover the whole surface, continue building the remainder inside the comp's world and design language.

## No image generation

State in one line that the visual-direction-by-generation step is skipped because the harness lacks native image generation, then proceed. The one-line announcement is required; it forces a conscious decision instead of letting the step quietly evaporate. The brief and its named anchor references are the contract.
